Most Poconos holiday searches end in the same two places: a hotel room that splits your family across a corridor, or a resort package priced per person. If you are eight people who would rather wake up in the same house on Christmas morning, this page is the practical version — what the house actually is, what the holiday nights cost, and the minimum stays you will run into.

[Slanted Stone Chalet](/) is a 2,900 square foot house in Pocono Pines with three bedrooms, three and a half bathrooms, and a kids' bunk room in a converted office. It sleeps eight.

## The holiday nights, and the minimum stay

The two things that catch people out at the holidays are the minimum stay and the price step.

**Christmas: 23–26 December is a 3-night minimum.** So is **New Year: 30 December – 2 January.** The nights on either side — 18–22 December and 27–29 December — take a 2-night booking. If you want Christmas Eve through Boxing Day, that works. If you want only Christmas Eve and Christmas Day, it does not, and no amount of asking changes it.

Holiday nights are also the most expensive of the year here, by a wide margin. Rather than print a number that will be stale by the time you read this, the [live rate table on the homepage](/#rates) pulls from our pricing system every night and now shows **Christmas & New Year as its own season**, separately from late fall. Check it there and you will see the real figure for the dates you want, not a brochure number.

Two practical notes. New Year's Eve prices at the very top of the year's range — the Christmas & New Year season is the only one that reaches it — and it books as part of a 3-night block, not alone. And the week between Christmas and New Year — 27, 28, 29 December — is a 2-night minimum at noticeably less than the Christmas and New Year peaks. If your dates are flexible, that mid-week gap is the value in the holiday fortnight.

## What the house gives you that a hotel does not

The case for a house at the holidays is mostly about the hours when nothing is scheduled.

A **gourmet kitchen** means Christmas dinner happens where you are staying, not at a restaurant seating at 4pm. The **game loft with shuffleboard** absorbs teenagers for an entire afternoon. The **stone firepit has a s'mores station**, which is the kind of thing that sounds like marketing until it is 6pm on 26 December and it is dark at four.

Then the two that sell this house in winter specifically: a **Finnish barrel sauna** and a **hot tub**. Sitting in either while it snows is the thing guests actually come back for. There is also a **ski lift chair swing** and forest views from every window, which in a Pocono winter means bare hardwood and, often enough, snow.

Three and a half bathrooms for eight people is the unglamorous number that matters most on Christmas morning.

For a holiday week, the useful ones are the ski mountains. **Jack Frost and Big Boulder are 20 minutes away**, and so is **Camelback**, which also runs snow tubing — the thing to do with a group that does not all ski. **Kalahari's indoor waterpark is 14 minutes** and runs at 84°F regardless of what December is doing outside, which is a reliable answer to a wet afternoon. **Tobyhanna State Park, 15 minutes**, is where you go for a cold, quiet walk around a lake when the house needs emptying for an hour.

**Mount Airy Casino is 19 minutes** and open on the nights when everything else is closed, which over the holidays is most of them.
