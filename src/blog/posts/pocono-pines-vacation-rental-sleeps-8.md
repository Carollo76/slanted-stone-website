The barrel sauna is the real thing — a proper Finnish-style setup outdoors, ideal after a cold day at Camelback Mountain (about 20 minutes away) or Jack Frost / Big Boulder (also 20 minutes). Heat up, cool down, repeat. It pairs well with the hot tub if your group likes to rotate.

### Hot Tub
Available year-round, and genuinely one of the best places to land after a long drive or a full day out. With forest on every side, it doesn't feel like you're soaking in a suburban backyard.

### Game Loft with Shuffleboard
The game loft is upstairs and gets heavy use on rainy afternoons or late evenings when nobody wants to drive anywhere. Shuffleboard specifically is the kind of game that pulls everyone in regardless of age — no experience required, competition gets surprisingly intense.

### Stone Firepit with S'mores Station
The outdoor firepit is set in stone and comes with a proper s'mores station. This is the detail that makes holiday weekends feel special — a place to gather after dinner that isn't just the living room couch. Kids love it, adults love it more once someone breaks out the good chocolate.

One of the more unusual features: a ski lift chair repurposed as a porch swing. It's a conversation piece, a great photo, and genuinely a comfortable place to drink your coffee and watch the fog move through the trees in the morning.

### Gourmet Kitchen
If your group is the type that actually cooks — holiday meals, big breakfasts before a ski day, late-night snacks — the kitchen handles it. There's enough counter space and equipment that multiple people can work in there without chaos.
