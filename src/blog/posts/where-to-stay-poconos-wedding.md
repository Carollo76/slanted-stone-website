If you have just been invited to a wedding in the Poconos, you have probably already discovered the two things everyone discovers: the venue's room block is smaller than the guest list, and what is left nearby is either expensive or a thirty-minute drive anyway.

This is a practical guide to the second part — where to actually stay — written by someone who hosts fifteen minutes from the middle of it.

## Drive times from the main Pocono wedding venues

Distances are to Pocono Pines, measured on Google Maps:

| Venue | Drive time |
|---|---|
| **Kalahari Resorts** (Pocono Manor) | **14 minutes** |
| **Stroudsmoor Country Inn** (Stroudsburg) | 28 minutes |
| **Shawnee Inn** (Shawnee on Delaware) | 32 minutes |
| **Skytop Lodge** (Skytop) | 34 minutes |

Being straight about that: if your wedding is at Stroudsmoor, Shawnee or Skytop, this is a half-hour drive, not a five-minute one. Whether that works depends on your group and your plans, and the rest of this page is about when it does.

If your wedding is at **Kalahari**, fourteen minutes is a genuinely short hop.

## When a house makes more sense than a room block

**When there are more than four of you.** A room block charges per room. A house charges once. Somewhere around five or six people the maths flips, and it keeps flipping further in your favour as the group grows.

**When the block sells out.** It usually does, and the people who book late end up scattered across three hotels in different towns. A house keeps everyone together, which for a family travelling in is often the entire point.

**When you are making a weekend of it.** Most people flying or driving into the Poconos for a Saturday wedding are there Friday to Sunday. That leaves two days to fill, and a hotel room is a poor place to fill them.

**When there is a morning after.** Eight people, one kitchen, coffee, and nobody has to be anywhere. That is a better Sunday than a hotel breakfast buffet at separate tables.

## The honest caveat: somebody has to drive

At 28 to 34 minutes from the Stroudsburg-area venues, this only works if you have a designated driver or a plan for cars.

Rideshare coverage in the Poconos is thin and gets thinner late at night — do not assume you will get a car at midnight from a venue in the woods. Plan the return trip before you need it.

If your group would rather not think about any of that, the venue's own rooms are worth what they cost. We would rather say so than pretend otherwise.

## What the house actually gives a wedding party

[Slanted Stone Chalet](/) is 2,900 square feet in Pocono Pines — three bedrooms plus a bunk room, three and a half baths, sleeping eight.

For a wedding weekend specifically:

- **Somewhere to get ready.** Three and a half baths and a lot of mirrors beats taking turns in a hotel bathroom.
- **A kitchen.** Wedding weekends are expensive enough without three restaurant meals a day for eight people.
- **A hot tub and a Finnish barrel sauna** for the morning after, which is the part nobody plans for and everybody needs.
- **A game loft with shuffleboard** for the Friday night when people arrive at different times.
- **A stone firepit** for the evening after, when the group wants to sit together and not be anywhere.
- **Forest on every side**, so nobody is worrying about noise.

## Two days to fill either side of the wedding

Most of what is worth doing is closer to us than to the venues:

- **Tobyhanna State Park**, 15 minutes — lake trails, easy walking, good for a hungover Sunday
- **Kalahari Waterpark**, 14 minutes — indoor and 84°F year-round, useful with kids in tow
- **Camelback Mountain**, 20 minutes — skiing in winter, mountain coaster and waterpark in summer
- **Mount Airy Casino**, 19 minutes — a place to send the group that wants a late night
- **Delaware Water Gap**, 30 minutes — the drive is genuinely beautiful and it is near Shawnee anyway

## Booking for a wedding weekend

Wedding dates get held early, and the Poconos wedding season runs May through October with a second cluster around the winter holidays. If you know the date, book well ahead — those are the weekends that go first here too.

Minimum stays are two nights most of the year and three over holiday weekends, which usually matches a wedding weekend anyway.
